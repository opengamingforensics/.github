## 🏛️ The Forensic Charter (不可妥协的法医宪章)

Modern free-to-play mobile gaming generates over **$100 Billion annually**. But behind the glossy renders, dynamic particle effects, and celebratory fanfare lies a darker mathematical reality: dynamic difficulty algorithms calibrated against player retention thresholds, obfuscated pseudorandom drop tables, and patents designed by neuroscientists to extract compliance rather than deliver joy.

**The Open Gaming Forensic Lab accepts ZERO commercial sponsorships from predatory gacha publishers or pay-to-win mobile titles.** All investigations are conducted with clean-room methodology:
1. **PATENT DECOMPILATION**: Auditing official filings from USPTO, WIPO, and international patent offices (EA, Activision Blizzard, Take-Two, Tencent).
2. **ALGORITHM REPRODUCIBILITY**: Open-source Monte Carlo simulations, network packet audits, and PRNG probability models.
3. **FINANCIAL TRACKING**: SEC Form 10-K filings, platform tolls, and offshore tax arbitrage.
4. **THE PANACEA**: Advocacy for true interactive art—premium, ethical, non-predatory indie masterpieces.
